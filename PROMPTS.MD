## Catálogo de Prompts: Tratamento de Imagens de Marmitas

Este documento armazena os comandos utilizados para padronizar e aprimorar a qualidade fotográfica das imagens de marmitas.

> **Observação Importante:** Para a execução de qualquer um dos prompts abaixo, é estritamente necessário enviar uma foto de uma marmita de exemplo (referência de cenário) para garantir o entendimento correto da composição.

---

### 1. Inserção de Múltiplas Marmitas no Cenário

**Objetivo:** Substituir o item da imagem de referência por um conjunto de várias marmitas extraídas de uma segunda imagem, organizando-as perfeitamente no novo cenário.

```text
I am sending you exactly TWO images with completely different purposes. Read carefully before generating:

📸 IMAGE 1 = BACKGROUND & STYLE REFERENCE ONLY
This image contains a wooden table with one meal box on it.
- USE the wooden table, lighting, shadows, camera angle, and overall photographic style
- IGNORE and DISCARD the meal box shown in this image — it must NOT appear in the final result
- Think of Image 1 as "the empty stage" — you are only borrowing the stage, not the actor

📸 IMAGE 2 = THE MEALS TO PLACE ON THE STAGE
This image contains multiple meal boxes (marmitas) grouped together.
- Take ALL the meal boxes from this image
- Place them onto the wooden table from Image 1
- Keep each meal box exactly as shown (same food, same container, same appearance)

YOUR TASK:
Generate ONE new image showing the wooden table from Image 1, with all the meal boxes from Image 2 arranged neatly on top of it. The meal box originally shown in Image 1 must be completely removed and replaced by the meals from Image 2.

ARRANGEMENT ON THE TABLE:
- Count the meal boxes in Image 2 and arrange them symmetrically:
  • 2 meals → 1 row of 2
  • 3 meals → 1 row of 3
  • 4 meals → 2x2 grid
  • 5 meals → 2 top + 3 bottom (centered)
  • 6 meals → 2x3 grid
- ALL meal containers must be the exact same size in the final image
- Equal spacing between containers (3-5% of frame width)
- Group centered on the table, occupying 80-85% of the frame
- Generous negative space around the group showing the wooden surface

MATCH IMAGE 1 EXACTLY FOR:
- Wood tone, grain, texture, and imperfections (same wooden table)
- Lighting direction (same light source and angle)
- Shadow softness and direction (all meals cast shadows in the same direction as Image 1)
- Color temperature and mood (warm ~5000-5200K)
- Camera angle (top-down tilted 15-20 degrees)
- Overall color grading and editorial quality

PRESERVE FROM IMAGE 2:
- The food in each container EXACTLY as shown — do not alter, add, remove, or reshape any ingredient, sauce, or portion
- The shape and appearance of each transparent plastic container
- Pixel-perfect food composition in every meal
- Match each meal to its original container — do not mix or swap foods
- Apply the same subtle color grading to all meals for visual consistency with Image 1

TECHNICAL STYLE:
- Square 1:1 aspect ratio
- Professional food photography, editorial magazine quality
- Canon EOS R5 with 50mm f/2.8 lens
- Photorealistic, indistinguishable from a real DSLR photograph
- NO plastic-looking surfaces, NO artificial glow, NO cartoon colors, NO collage feel
- Must look like all the marmitas from Image 2 were physically placed on the table from Image 1 and photographed in one single real shot

FINAL VERIFICATION BEFORE GENERATING:
✅ Only the table/background from Image 1 is used
✅ The meal box from Image 1 is completely removed
✅ ALL meals from Image 2 are placed on that table
✅ Total meal count in final image = meal count in Image 2 (nothing from Image 1)

```

### 2. Composição de Duas Marmitas Lado a Lado

**Objetivo:** Unificar duas fotos distintas de marmitas isoladas (enviadas separadamente) em um único cenário rústico, mantendo proporções, iluminação e alinhamento perfeitos.

```text
I am sending you two images, each containing one Brazilian meal box (marmita) in a transparent plastic container.

Your task: Create ONE single new image that combines both meal boxes side by side on the same rustic wooden table, as if they were photographed together in the same scene.

COMPOSITION REQUIREMENTS:
- Place both meal containers side by side, horizontally aligned
- Both containers must be the EXACT same size in the final image (identical scale and perspective)
- Both containers must sit on the same horizontal plane (same "table level")
- Equal spacing: small gap between the two containers (about 3-5% of the frame width)
- Both containers centered as a pair within the frame
- The pair should occupy approximately 80% of the frame width
- Square 1:1 aspect ratio
- Generous negative space above and below showing the wooden surface

BACKGROUND (same for both):
- Rustic light oak wooden table surface
- Warm honey-toned wood with visible natural grain
- Subtle knots, small dark spots, natural imperfections
- Matte finish, no varnish shine
- Continuous, seamless wood surface behind both containers (not two separate backgrounds merged)

LIGHTING (unified across both):
- Single light source: soft natural daylight from the upper area
- Warm color temperature around 5000-5200K
- Both containers must have shadows falling in the EXACT SAME DIRECTION
- Identical shadow softness and length on both
- Subtle contact shadow beneath each container to ground them realistically
- Even, appetizing illumination on both meals — no container brighter than the other

CAMERA ANGLE:
- Top-down angle, slightly tilted at approximately 15-20 degrees
- Both containers viewed from the exact same angle and perspective
- No distortion or perspective mismatch between the two

FOOD PRESERVATION:
- Keep the food in BOTH containers EXACTLY as shown in the original images
- Do not alter, add, remove, or reshape any ingredient, sauce, or portion
- Preserve pixel-perfect food composition in both meals
- Enhance colors subtly on both (natural vibrancy, never oversaturated) — apply the SAME color grading to both

TECHNICAL STYLE:
- Professional food photography, editorial magazine quality
- Shot on Canon EOS R5 with 50mm f/2.8 lens
- Sharp focus on both containers
- Photorealistic, indistinguishable from a real DSLR photograph
- NO plastic-looking surfaces, NO artificial glow, NO cartoon colors
- Must look like both marmitas were physically placed on the same table and photographed together in one shot — not a collage or a composite

The final image must look 100% like a real photograph taken by a professional food photographer, with perfect visual consistency between both meal boxes, ready for use in a product catalog showcasing meal combos or daily specials.

```

### 3. Melhoria e Padronização de Uma Única Marmita

**Objetivo:** Transferir a qualidade fotográfica, a iluminação e o cenário de uma imagem de referência profissional para a foto de uma nova marmita que precisa ser editada.

```text
I am sending you two images:

IMAGE 1 (REFERENCE): A professional photo of a lasagna meal box on a rustic wooden table. Use this image ONLY as a visual style guide.

IMAGE 2 (TO EDIT): A photo of a different meal box that needs to be edited.

Your task: Edit IMAGE 2 to match the exact visual style of IMAGE 1.

Match perfectly from IMAGE 1:
- The rustic light oak wooden background (same wood tone, grain, and texture)
- The camera angle (slightly tilted top-down, ~15-20 degrees)
- The lighting direction, softness, and warm color temperature (~5000-5200K)
- The soft contact shadow beneath the container
- The square 1:1 composition with the container centered
- The container size relative to the frame (~60-65%)
- The overall mood, color grading, and professional editorial quality

Preserve from IMAGE 2:
- The food EXACTLY as it appears — do not alter, add, remove, or reshape any ingredient, sauce, or portion
- The transparent plastic container shape and size
- Pixel-perfect food composition

Only enhance in IMAGE 2:
- Subtle color vibrancy of the food (natural, never oversaturated)
- Lighting to match IMAGE 1
- Replace the original background with the wooden surface from IMAGE 1

Final result: A photo that looks like it was taken in the same photo session as IMAGE 1, by the same photographer, for the same product catalog. Photorealistic, indistinguishable from a real DSLR photograph. No AI artifacts, no plastic look, no artificial glow.

```
